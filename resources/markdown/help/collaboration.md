# Collaboration

Assessments are built together — in a workshop, or by a team over time.
Everyone with access can have an assessment open at once.

## Presence

Avatars show who has the assessment open, and a coloured ring marks the node
each person has focused. Other people's changes appear live, with a grouped
notice ("Dana made 6 changes") rather than one per change. Your own changes
never notify you.

## One editor per node

Opening a node's pop-up reserves that node for you. Anyone else who opens it
sees it **read-only**, with *Currently being edited by \<name\>* in its
header, and its card shows a lock with your name. When you close the pop-up
it becomes editable for them straight away. Other nodes stay free, so
several people can work on the same assessment side by side.

If your tab closes or your connection drops, the reservation expires on its
own within a minute.

## Saving and conflicts

Changes save as you type; the toolbar shows **Saving… / Saved / Conflict**.
If a node changed on the server since you loaded it, your save is refused
rather than overwriting it, and you choose **Keep mine**, **Take theirs** or
**Cancel**. Nothing is silently overwritten or discarded.

## Comments, mentions and assignees

- **Comments** on a node are threaded and can be resolved. Type `@` and a
  name to mention a collaborator.
- **Assignees** make someone responsible for a node. It appears on their
  dashboard under *Assigned to me*, with what it still needs. Assigning grants
  no access: only people the assessment is shared with can be assigned.
- **Notifications** (bell, in the sidebar) tell you about mentions,
  assignments and finished AI drafts. The dashboard also lists your
  mentions.

## Who sees what

Presence and live updates go only to people allowed to view that assessment.
Someone reading through a read-only link does not appear in presence and
receives nothing that identifies anyone else.
