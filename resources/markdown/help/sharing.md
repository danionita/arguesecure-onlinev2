# Sharing and roles

Every assessment is private by default: only its owner and the instance's
administrators can open it. **Share**, in the bar above any view, lets others
in.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | View the assessment. |
| **Commenter** | View and comment. |
| **Editor** | View, comment and edit nodes, links and values. |
| **Manager** | Everything an editor can, plus share, change settings and scales, and delete the assessment. |

The owner and administrators can always do everything. Only they can transfer
ownership or erase a deleted assessment permanently; a manager can delete
one and restore it. The share dialog lists every
collaborator with their role and who invited them; removing someone takes
effect immediately.

*My assessments* lists the ones you own. Those others have shared with you
are under **Shared with me**, each with its owner's name.

## Public assessments

Making an assessment **public** lets anyone with its address view it, even
without an account. Public is always **read-only**: it never allows
commenting or editing. To let someone edit, give them an account and a
role. Administrators can turn public assessments off for the whole instance.

## The read-only link

Each assessment can also have one **revocable read-only link**: anyone who
holds it can view the assessment without an account: opening the link goes
straight to the assessment, read-only. The owner or a manager
can create, copy and revoke it. Revoking stops the link working at once, for
good; a new link is a new address.

## Templates are different

A template is visible to every account, for starting new assessments, but
using one gives you a copy — nobody gets access to the template itself. Only
administrators can publish templates. See [Templates](/help/templates).
